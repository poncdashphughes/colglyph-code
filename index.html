ColGlyphCode

Encode any file into colour.
Decode it back.

ColGlyphCode stores data as a grid of 16 colours — each cell encodes a 4-bit nibble, protected by Reed–Solomon error correction. Upload a file, choose a paper size or screen size, and the system calculates how much data fits. The result is a camera-readable colour glyph that reconstructs your original file, even when parts of it are smudged, glared, or damaged.

From A4 pages holding kilobytes to business card labels holding a few bytes, the same encoding works everywhere. Print it and scan it back — or present it on a screen and let a phone collect the file live, with no network between them.
